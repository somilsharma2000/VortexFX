Scoring System: Fair Performance Score (FPS)

Cross-account equity normalization ensures a $5k account competes with absolute fairness against a $200k account.

NPL FORMULA FPS = R% × CF × RAM
R% — Normalized Return

(Net Profit / Starting Capital) × 100. Percentage gain ensures fair scale parity.

CF — Consistency Factor

(Green Trading Days / Total Active Days) × Concentration Penalty.

RAM — Risk Adjustment

Multiplier between 0.00 and 1.25 based on peak historical drawdown control.
