Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

1. Data We Collect

FORTREX FX collects the following data to operate the Platform:

1.1 Discord Data. When you sign in with Discord OAuth, we collect your Discord username, user ID, and avatar. We do NOT collect your Discord password, messages, or Discord activity.

1.2 Broker Account Number. When you link your MT4/MT5 account, we store your broker account number. We do NOT have access to your broker password, trading funds, or transaction history. We cannot access, control, or modify your broker account.

1.3 Tournament Data. We store your tournament participation data, including starting balance, final equity, and calculated ROI. This data is submitted by the Platform administrator based on verification of your broker account performance.

1.4 Platform Activity. We track your Platform activity including tournaments entered, REX balance, referral activity, and page interactions for the purpose of operating the Platform.

1.5 Technical Data. We collect basic technical data including IP address, browser type, and device information for security and analytics purposes.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage your FORTREX FX account
  • To verify tournament participation and calculate results
  • To display your public trader profile (username, tournament results, stats)
  • To track and distribute REX reward points
  • To operate the referral program
  • To prevent fraud, multi-accounting, and platform abuse
  • To improve Platform features and user experience

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Your broker account password or login credentials
  • Access to your trading funds or positions
  • Your Discord private messages or activity outside FORTREX FX
  • Your financial information beyond what you voluntarily provide (MT4/MT5 account number)
  • Personal documents (KYC is handled by your broker, not by FORTREX FX)

4. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties. We may share data only in the following circumstances:

  • With our IB partner (regulated broker) for the purpose of verifying trading volume and eligibility
  • With legal authorities if required by law or court order
  • With service providers (e.g., hosting, analytics) under strict data protection agreements

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, we will remove your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt out of non-essential communications
  • Export your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, contact: privacy@fortrexfx.com

7. Cookies

The Platform uses essential cookies for authentication and session management. We do NOT use advertising or tracking cookies. Analytics cookies, if used, are anonymized and do not identify individual users.

8. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission, secure authentication via Discord OAuth, and access controls. However, no system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute data security.

9. Children's Privacy

The Platform is not intended for users under 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we become aware that a minor has registered, we will delete their account and data.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the Platform or Discord community. Continued use of the Platform constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests: privacy@fortrexfx.com
