FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Knowledge Base

How do I participate in tournaments?
+
Register your account, complete simple verification, select an open tournament from the arena list, and click Enter. You will be assigned a tournament demo account with identical starting capital.
What is Rex AI and how does it help?
+
Rex AI is our proprietary trading analysis engine. It provides real-time pattern recognition, risk warnings, and tournament position insights directly on your trading dashboard.
Are tournament funds real or virtual?
+
All tournaments use virtual funds in a simulated environment to ensure a fair playing field. Prize pools are paid out in real crypto or fiat currency to top performers.
How are tournament rebates calculated?
+
Rebates are calculated based on your total trading volume executed during active tournaments. Higher recruitment tiers boost your rebate percentage up to an additional 25%.
What happens if there is a tie on the leaderboard?
+
In the event of equal ROI percentages, ties are broken first by lowest maximum drawdown, and second by total executed trade volume.
Can I enter multiple tournaments simultaneously?
+
Yes, Commanders can participate in up to 3 concurrent active tournaments, managed through separate sub-terminals.
