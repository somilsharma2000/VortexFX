QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

How do I join a tournament? +
Register on Fortex FX with your Discord account, link your XM MT4/MT5 account number, and meet the entry criteria (minimum deposit set by admin). Once eligible, you're automatically entered into the next tournament.
Why are results sealed until the last day? +
Sealed results keep the competition fair and exciting. Without live rankings, traders can't adjust their strategy based on others' performance. You trade your own game and find out where you stand on reveal day.
How is ROI calculated? +
ROI = (Final Equity - Starting Balance) / Starting Balance × 100. Your starting balance is recorded when the tournament begins, and your final equity is checked on the last day.
What markets can I trade in tournaments? +
Forex and Crypto. Primarily forex pairs (EUR/USD, GBP/USD, XAU/USD, etc.) with crypto available as secondary instruments. All trading happens on your own MT4/MT5 terminal.
How are prizes paid out? +
Prizes are distributed in REX — the Fortex FX platform currency. REX can be used for rebates, rewards, and other platform features. Prize distribution happens after the admin verifies final results on reveal day.
Do I need to pay to use Fortex FX? +
No. Fortex FX is completely free. You trade with your own funds in your own broker account. The platform doesn't charge any subscription or entry fee.
