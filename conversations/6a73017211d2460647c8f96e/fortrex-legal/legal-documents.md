## DOCUMENT 3: COMPETITION RULES & SCORING

### FORTREX Competition Official Rules

#### 1. Competition Structure

**1.1 Season Model**: Competitions run in monthly seasons. Each season contains 2 tournaments (bi-weekly).

**1.2 Brackets**:
- Lightweight: $10K – $25K account size
- Middleweight: $50K – $100K account size
- Heavyweight: $200K+ account size

**1.3** Bracket assignment is based on the trader's account size, not self-selection.

#### 2. Composite Scoring System

**2.1** Final score = (ROI% × 0.40) + (Consistency × 0.30) + (Risk Management × 0.30)

**2.2 ROI%**: (Current Equity - Starting Balance) / Starting Balance × 100

**2.3 Consistency Score** (0-100):
- Daily return consistency (standard deviation of daily returns)
- Win rate (%)
- Profit factor (gross profit / gross loss)

**2.4 Risk Management Score** (0-100):
- Maximum drawdown vs. allowed limit
- Maximum daily loss vs. allowed limit
- Average lot size consistency

**2.5** Scoring weights are configurable by administration and published before each tournament.

#### 3. Verification Methods

**3.1 Tier 1 (Preferred)**: API connection to prop firm/broker for automatic trade verification

**3.2 Tier 2 (Backup)**: Verified screenshot submission with AI-assisted fraud detection (metadata analysis, image manipulation detection, cross-reference with broker public data)

**3.3 Tier 3 (Manual)**: Admin review for flagged accounts

#### 4. Disqualification Rules

**4.1** Maximum daily loss: 5% of starting balance (configurable)
**4.2** Maximum overall drawdown: 10% of starting balance (configurable)
**4.3** Minimum trading days: 5 (configurable)
**4.4** Prohibited strategies: Latency arbitrage, news straddling, copy trading, account sharing
**4.5** Tie-breakers (in order): Lowest drawdown → Highest profit factor → Highest win rate

#### 5. Prize Distribution

**5.1** Prize distribution is published before each tournament.
**5.2** All prize recipients must complete KYC Level 2+.
**5.3** Prizes are distributed within 14 business days of verification.
**5.4** Administration may adjust prize pools at any time before tournament start.
