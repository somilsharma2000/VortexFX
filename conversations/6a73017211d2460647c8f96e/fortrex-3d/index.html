Questions, Answered

FAQ

What is FORTREX? ▼
FORTREX is a professional trading community that organizes skill-based competitions for verified traders. We partner with brokers and prop firms to offer real rewards — cash and funded accounts — to traders who demonstrate superior trading skill.
How do I enter a competition? ▼
Open a broker account using our partner link, deposit $100, and complete verification. Once verified, you can join any live arena. For prop firm arenas, purchase a funded account using our partner code.
How are winners decided? ▼
Winners are determined by a composite score: 40% ROI (percentage-based, not raw profit), 30% consistency (daily returns, win rate, profit factor), and 30% risk management (drawdown adherence, lot size discipline). This ensures fair competition across different account sizes.
What is the $3/lot rebate? ▼
Every verified FORTREX trader earns $3 back for every lot they trade. This is funded by our broker partnership — we share our affiliate commission with you. Trade more, earn more. No cap. No catch.
What is Rex? ▼
Rex is FORTREX's loyalty reward point system. Earn Rex through daily logins, referrals, quizzes, and tournament participation. Redeem Rex for lucky draw tickets, tournament passes, and more. Rex is not a cryptocurrency and cannot be converted to cash.
Is this gambling? ▼
No. FORTREX competitions are skill-based — winners are determined by trading performance metrics (ROI, consistency, risk management), not by chance. Users are responsible for complying with their local jurisdiction's laws.
How often do tournaments run? ▼
Tournaments run every two weeks — two per month. Each tournament has its own prize pool, brackets, and rules. Season finales bring the biggest rewards.
Why do I need to join Discord? ▼
FORTREX Citadel on Discord is our community hub. Tournament prize pool reveals, live announcements, trader discussions, and support all happen there. Your FORTREX identity and Rex balance sync across both platforms.
